# Surface brief: Restock home (app shell)

Scope: the one home screen (Needs you, Pantry, Handled), item detail sheet, rules sheet, and the labeled demo layer. Visitor mode: Operate. Phone first; desktop shows the phone column centered with the demo panel beside it.

Audience and job: the person keeping a household stocked, glancing between other things; secondary, an evaluator with 60 seconds. Constraints: never busy, never a chatbot. All data synthetic and labeled.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The household list pad, kept for you. The normal state is a blank sheet that says everything is stocked. Refuses the delivery-app grid (thumbnails, green buttons) and the chat window.

OWN-WORLD: Cool fridge-enamel ground. Canary pad sheets hold asks, with a perforated top edge. Ballpoint blue is the only ink. Pale-blue rule lines. Supply drawn as the rule line itself, one ink at five densities, weight rising with urgency. Pencil-grey strike-through for handled. Magnet red only for blocked. One grotesk, tabular figures.

STORY: At a glance: nothing needs you, or one decision does, already worked out. Everything handled shows the rule that allowed it. Rules read as sentences. Evaluators break the agent and watch the spend lock hold.

FIRST VIEWPORT: At 390px: top, a canary sheet held by a red magnet dot, the decision as a 22px line, reason in small print, two or three outlined answer buttons (the primary action). Below, "Pantry": ruled rows, name, store, days left on the rule. A slim "Demo" bar is fixed at the bottom.

FORM: The Fridge List Pad, position 3 of my ordered list, seed key 3ace9194. Signature interaction: answering tears the sheet off along its perforation, revealing the next sheet or the blank pad.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
